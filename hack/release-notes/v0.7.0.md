## Changes

vens now scores each of OWASP's 16 factors on its own, and `config.yaml` has a version.

### Breaking changes
- **`config.yaml` needs `schema_version: 2`.** A file without it is refused at load, before any LLM call, with a pointer to the [migration notes](https://venslabs.github.io/vens/reference/config-schema/#migrating). Every existing key keeps its name, values and validation.
- **Unknown keys are refused.** A misspelled key used to be ignored: `compliance_requirement:` (singular) dropped your frameworks without a word. It now fails at load and names the key and its line.

### New in `config.yaml`
- `listener`: `present`, `absent` or `unknown` (default). Does this deployment accept connections others open? `present` and `absent` are shown to the model with the rest of the deployment; `unknown` is not shown.
- `data_subjects`: `none`, `one_individual`, `hundreds`, `thousands`, `millions`. How many people's personal data the service can read. It caps Privacy violation; never sent to the model.
- `detection`: `network`, `host` and `application`, each `not_logged`, `logged` or `logged_and_reviewed`, and `active_in_application` for `application`. What records an attempt on each channel, across the whole service. Declare all three, or leave the key out. It sets Intrusion detection; never sent to the model.
- `traceability`: `none`, `per_request`, `per_individual`. What the service records of who did what, as it is, not as required. It sets Loss of accountability with the model's answer; never sent to the model.
- `reachability`: a list of entries, each with one selector, `cve` or `component`, `reachable` and a `provenance`. An entry declaring a CVE not reachable, by its id or by a component equal to every package the scan lists for it, publishes the CVE `not_affected`, `code_not_reachable`, with a `detail` citing the entry; an entry declaring it reachable, by its id or one of its packages, vetoes that. It moves no score; never sent to the model.

### How vens rates a CVE
- vens asks the model one question per model-answered factor, up to 9 of the 16 and only when the factor's contract requires it, each with a closed list of named answers, and computes every level, the severity and the score in Go. The published vector carries 16 levels OWASP defines; the severity is OWASP's matrix; the score is likelihood × impact, a vens number, not OWASP's.
- Skill level, Ease of discovery and Awareness are 9 for every finding, so likelihood is never LOW and `info` never occurs: a Low severity needs a LOW impact.
- The impact is the larger of the technical and the business impact: on OWASP's own example vens says High where OWASP says Low (#340).
- Loss of confidentiality, integrity and availability are not asked: vens reads them from the worst `C`, `I` and `A` of the CVE's usable CVSS v3 vectors, confidentiality in the column `data_sensitivity` picks. `N` gives 2, 1 and 1; `L` gives 2 (6 on high or critical data), 3 and 5; `H` gives 6 (7), 7 and 7. A CVE with no usable v3 vector takes 9 for each, counted as `cvss_v3_missing`. `availability_requirement` no longer moves any score, and Privacy violation is asked whatever the vectors give `C`.
- No question asks whether a CVE is reachable, and nothing in `notes` publishes a VEX status: only a `reachability` entry does, and the CVE keeps the rating its answers give, with no level lowered and no severity capped. vens reads no upstream VEX.
- Compliance frameworks no longer add 2; the model judges the violation.
- A CVE with no description in the scanner's report is not asked Motive, Opportunity or Ease of exploit: they take their worst level. A title alone does not count as a description.
- A flagged CVE's adjustment asks Opportunity alone, and only of a CVE with a description.

### Your scores will change
- **Every existing config scores LAC 7 or 9 until `traceability` is declared.** `audit_requirement` states a requirement, not what is in place: it no longer moves any score, and vens warns when it is set and `traceability` is not.
- **Intrusion detection is 9 on every finding until you declare `detection`.** `ids`, `siem` and `edr` say a tool exists, not that this service's attempts are recorded, so they give no credit; vens warns when one is on and `detection` is absent.
- **Privacy violation has no cap until you declare `data_subjects`**: the model's answer stands.
- Declaring the worst case (`traceability: none`, every channel `not_logged`, `data_subjects: millions`) gives the same levels, without the warning.
- `waf`, `ddos_protection`, `segmentation`, `zero_trust` and `antivirus` are shown, with their scope, only where the model judges how easy the exploit is here.

### Runs and output
- `--llm-batch-size` counts CVEs, each sent once per question asked about it. On the demo scan at batch 10, in the internet-facing context with detection undeclared, a run makes 95 calls instead of 13 [stale: counted with the reachability and the three impact questions; cost and time: to measure before the release].
- Each vulnerability carries the scoring method's version [carrier to settle and test through Dependency-Track's VEX import before the release: `vulnerabilities[].tools`, or the vens version in `metadata.tools`].
- `--strict` fails a run that would use a worst-case fallback. A scan holding a CVE with no usable CVSS v3 vector or no description in the scanner's report cannot pass it; the error lists every such CVE.
- A reply vens cannot read no longer stops the run at once: the CVEs it leaves unanswered are asked again, once, as when a reply leaves one out, and the run stops only if they are still unanswered. Each such reply is counted as `reply_unreadable`.
- `--debug-dir` also writes `answers.json`: per CVE, each answer as the model wrote it, how vens read it, the level and where it came from (`cvss` for the three technical impacts, with the value read), the `reachability` entries that hit it and the status they publish, and the counters; for the run, its defects.
- Every rating's severity is `low`, `medium`, `high` or `critical`. With vens-action's `fail-on-severity` set, a vulnerability with no OWASP rating, or a severity other than critical, high, medium, low or info, fails the gate [vens-action #13, once merged].

This scoring did not pass its measurement (#310: step 5 and a pilot). It ships for its legal vectors and recomputable scores. The figures below compare it with the previous scoring (`main` at 43ab10b, which is v0.6.0 plus commits that do not touch scoring) on the demo scan with gpt-5.4-mini, two runs per context, so they are exploratory:
- A CVE judged not reachable can still rate high or critical on an internet-facing service: 53 high and 10 critical out of 108 such ratings. Among those 63 the likelihood is 5.75 or more (6.25 or more for 49), because Skill level, Ease of discovery and Awareness are fixed at their worst level, Size is 9 from the config and Intrusion detection was answered 8 or 9. The business factors are still asked as if the CVE were exploited.
- Scores spread less than with the previous scoring: a run's spread is 0.68 of it on internal-api and 0.59 on internet-facing.
- On internal-api the same model changes a CVE's severity more often from one run to the next: band agreement 0.73, against 0.84 before.
- The reachability answer changes between runs for 12 to 19% of CVEs. A CVE without a description is often judged not reachable (6 or 9 out of 10) with nothing in the facts to support it.
- A run makes 5.5 to 7.9 times the previous scoring's calls, about 3.4 to 4.6 times the cost (estimated).
- The config schema breaks (see Breaking changes).
- Not run with claude-haiku-4-5.

### Migrating
1. Add `schema_version: 2` at the top.
2. Declare `traceability`, `detection` and `data_subjects` for what is in place. vens does not derive them from `audit_requirement` or `controls`.
3. Set `listener: absent` for a job or client that accepts no connection.
4. Run once and read the warnings.

A schema-2 file still loads on vens v0.6 and earlier, which ignore unknown keys and score it the old way: upgrade every runner that reads it.

Details: [Migrating from vens v0.6](https://venslabs.github.io/vens/reference/config-schema/#migrating), [What changed in v0.7.0](https://venslabs.github.io/vens/concepts/cvss-vs-owasp/#what-changed-in-v070).
